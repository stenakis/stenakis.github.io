My way of thinking into MaisUno+1's task to improve the arrangement of infomration into their website.

Timeline 2021-2023
Tools used Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Photoshop

About

Ellinikon Vintage and Ellinikon Seaside, the only official nightclubs in Aegina, Greece, are renowned both locally and beyond the island. Remvi, recognized as one of the most chic cafes and brunch spots at the port of Aegina, also benefits from a strong social media presence. Given this context, it is essential to develop a visual identity that aligns with each business's unique style and messaging. In conclusion, each business is approached with a distinct style, resulting in a cohesive and visually appealing presentation.

Ellinikon Vintage and Ellinikon Seaside typically operate independently but occasionally both are open simultaneously. The identity of Ellinikon Vintage is characterized by bold colors, strong typography, and vintage imagery. In contrast, Ellinikon Seaside emphasizes glamour and vibrant colors. Both entities share a common principle: events are communicated with a prominent, logo-like title, followed by additional information presented with lower hierarchy.

Remvi is a sister business to the Ellinikon brand, owned by the same person. Its unique brunch establishment demanded a sophisticated approach to its social media identity. Primarily hosting weekly events and special holiday dinners, Remvi adopts a refined and premium style. The color palette is inspired by the interior decor, emphasizing gold and olive green hues. The design prominently features minimal script typography, ensuring clarity while allowing essential details to stand out. This cohesive aesthetic conveys an overall impression of sleek, high-end service, underscoring the delicacy and elegance that Remvi offers.
